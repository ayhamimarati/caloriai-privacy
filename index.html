AI Calorie Tracker - Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

CalorIAI is an AI-powered calorie and nutrition tracking application developed by Atlas Digital AI. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process and protect user information.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, the application may process:

  • Email address and account information.
  • Weight, height and gender.
  • Nutrition and personal fitness goals.
  • Food photos submitted for AI analysis.
  • Food journal and nutritional information.
  • Purchase information and analysis credits.
  • Technical information necessary for providing and securing the service.

3. How We Use Information

  • Provide and maintain application services.
  • Manage user accounts.
  • Analyze food photos using artificial intelligence.
  • Estimate calories and nutritional values.
  • Track nutrition and weight progress.
  • Manage purchases and analysis credits.
  • Protect the application against fraud and abuse.

4. Camera and Photos

CalorIAI accesses the camera or selected photos when the user chooses to use these features. Meal photos submitted for AI analysis are transmitted to an online processing service to estimate nutritional information.

Users should avoid submitting photos containing unnecessary personal or sensitive information.

5. Third-Party Services

CalorIAI may process information through the following service providers:

  • Google Firebase - authentication and cloud services.
  • Google Play Billing - in-app purchases.
  • Cloudflare - backend infrastructure.
  • OpenAI API - artificial intelligence and food image analysis.

OpenAI does not use API data for model training by default unless data sharing has been explicitly enabled.

Additional information about these providers:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI API Data Controls
  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy

6. Payments

In-app purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. Purchase records and transaction identifiers may be used to verify purchases and manage analysis credits.

7. Data Storage and Security

Application information may be stored locally on the user's device or processed through cloud services, depending on the feature used.

We use security measures intended to protect user information, including encrypted HTTPS connections for online services. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

Personal information is retained as necessary to provide application services, manage accounts, comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud.

OpenAI API abuse monitoring logs may be retained for up to 30 days under default settings, with possible legal and safety exceptions. Additional retention may depend on the API features and configuration used.

9. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their account and associated personal information by contacting:

Supportatlasdigitalai@gmail.com

Please include the email address associated with your CalorIAI account and specify the deletion request. Do not send your password.

Requests may require identity verification. Certain information may be retained when necessary to comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud or resolve disputes.

10. User Rights

Depending on applicable law, users may request access to, correction of or deletion of their personal information by contacting the developer.

11. Medical Disclaimer

Calorie and nutritional values provided by CalorIAI are estimates generated for informational purposes.

CalorIAI is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment or professional medical advice.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features, data processing practices or legal requirements change.
